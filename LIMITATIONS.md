# What the data can and cannot show

Please read this before reusing the data or citing numbers from it.

**The data show what an outside observer could establish, not how AI or cloud dependencies are distributed in the
Danish public sector.** Each step of the data collection depended on information being published or on someone
being willing and able to answer:

1. The register is built from what is findable, through public sources and contact with authorities. Systems that
   are publicised (signature projects, health systems listed in the Nordic AI Health Map, developers with
   informative websites) are more likely to be included than internal tools or pilots that were quietly closed.
2. Of the 364 systems in use or planned, a developer is identified for 270 and a private company developer for 182
   or 183, depending on the coding used (see the figure notes).
3. A cloud provider or hosting arrangement is identified for 108 systems, 56 of them privately developed. Most of
   this information came from direct contact with public authorities or vendors, rarely from public documentation.

**"Not found" is not one thing.** It can mean that the information is not public, that we did not ask, that we
asked and got no reply or a refusal, or that a developer did not know which subcontractors its system relies on.
The data do not yet distinguish these.

**Group differences partly reflect access.** Cloud information was found more often for systems built by public
bodies than for systems from private companies, but public authorities were also contacted more often. Comparisons
between developer types or sectors should not be read as differences in opacity alone.

**Concentration on Microsoft** is concentration among the systems where cloud information could be obtained.
Developers building on hyperscalers often publish sub-processor lists, which may make them easier to identify.

**Risk category and LLM** are not the authors' coding. Risk category comes from external sources (mainly the Nordic
AI Health Map for health systems, and partly KL's AI-landkort) on different scales; "Lav risiko" is not an EU AI Act
category. LLM is filled in for few systems. Their missing values reflect which source covered a system, not opacity.

**The register changes.** Systems are renamed, merged, added and closed between versions. Numbers in the article refer
to the snapshot of 23 November 2026.
